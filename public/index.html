First run

  1. Add your country, age and other reusable information under My profile.
  2. Use Try local practice to learn the review flow without opening an external survey.
  3. Configure Eureka and the domains you recognize under Connections & setup.
  4. Start a survey. Sign in manually in the browser if prompted, then Resume.
  5. When a question needs your input, answer in the review panel or use the ChatGPT handoff.

Keeping it running

Leave the laptop powered, awake and connected. The background launcher can restart the local app after a crash; operating-system login startup is optional. Browsers need your logged-in desktop session. Closing the laptop or losing internet will interrupt progress.

Your data

Settings, SQLite memory and a dedicated browser profile stay on this computer. Stop the app before making a backup. Browser sign-ins should be established separately on each laptop.

Compatibility

Native radio buttons, checkboxes, dropdowns, text and numeric inputs, scales, labelled matrices and conditional questions are supported. Embedded frames and unrecognized custom widgets pause for manual review. Eureka's third-party surveys can differ, so live compatibility is not guaranteed.

ChatGPT

The handoff works with your normal account, but it is manual. ChatGPT access and usage limits are determined by your plan. This application never promises unlimited messages or uploads.
